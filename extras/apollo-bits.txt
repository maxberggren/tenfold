# Chosen for 12.6 MB (from the published 66) by how much each layer, alone

# from the published weights' by 45 dB, about as much as two
# implementations of the float network differ on an MP3, and its distance
# from the lossless originals of eight test clips (log-spectral, above and
# below the codec's cutoff, MP3 at 128 and 64 kb/s) is the same to 0.01 dB.
# Layers not listed (norms, biases, the rotary tables) stay as published.
