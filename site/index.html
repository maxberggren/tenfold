An instrument played with all ten fingers on the trackpad.

Drums, bass, chords, lead, arp, atmosphere, risers and your own voice, each a page of the trackpad, always in key and in time. Play, press Space, and it loops. On the Song page your loops become a track, with builds and drops.

Install
curl -fsSL https://maxberggren.github.io/tenfold/install.sh | sh
  • Paste it in a terminal. For Arch-based systems: Omarchy, Arch, and Arch Linux ARM on Asahi Macs (x86_64 and arm64).
  • It installs the latest release with pacman, checked against its checksum, and Qt 6 and PipeWire with it.
  • A udev rule lets you read your own trackpad without a password. Nothing else on your system changes.
  • Read the script first, or download the package yourself.
macOS is coming. tenfold reads every finger on the trackpad, with its pressure and shape, and plays sound with no delay you can hear. Each system does that its own way, so macOS needs a port of its own, not just a build. Watch the project to hear when it's ready.

How it plays
1 · Play

Ten fingers

Every page is the trackpad: pads, notes, chords and your voice. Pressure, glides and twists all play.

2 · Space

It loops

Space keeps what you just played, on the bars. Again, and you layer on top.

0 · Song

A track

Turn the loops into sections, builds and drops, then record it. A tutorial walks you through it.
